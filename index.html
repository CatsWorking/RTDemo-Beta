About Project:

This project started as more of a proof of concept, probably was more of a joke since i literally wrote an essay on the first or second day back to school about this, being "Ray Tracing VS Rasterization", Since then, I have learnt a lot about Ray Tracing itself after making it myself in HTML and JS.

I had 3 different recodes for this, the first time i did it, it turned out nicely, but the camera ended up breaking and when I tried to implement ambient occlusion itself, the renderer shat itself, i really don't know why but i guess thats what life is, second time it did end up really good, and i used features from that one to implement into here, like the Volumetric Lighting that i didn't get to implement, it looked a lot like 90s CGI so i scrapped it, and you are here at the third recode, with all the features from the older versions.

About Ray Tracing:

Ray tracing is a rendering technique that simulates the behavior of light in a 3D scene by tracing the path of light rays as they interact with objects. This approach allows for realistic lighting effects, including shadows, reflections, and refractions, this demo shows off how it can work in a browser and is optimized HEAVILY for a gtx 1070 and is made for browsers and lower-end hardware for benchmarking, and fun
